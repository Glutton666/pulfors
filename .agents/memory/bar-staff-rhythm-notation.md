---
name: Bar staff rhythm notation
description: Product rules for converting a Bar beat denominator and subdivision count into simplified standard notation.
---

For subdivision counts 1–9, use the greatest power of two not exceeding the count as the standard division bucket. Multiply that bucket by the beat denominator to obtain the displayed note value. Non-power-of-two counts are tuplets; use beams when the resulting note value has flags and a hooked bracket otherwise.

**Why:** Beam count alone is insufficient. A denominator-2 single subdivision is a half note and must also use an open notehead, or the rendered duration contradicts the computed value.

**How to apply:** Keep stems upward in the single-voice Bar display. Show numbers only for 3, 5, 6, 7, and 9, centered above the beam or bracket; 2, 4, and 8 remain unnumbered regular divisions.
